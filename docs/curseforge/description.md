# Forever Data Bar

One thin strip across the whole width of the screen, at the top or the bottom edge, with what you check all the time, for **WoW: Forever**. English, Deutsch, Español, Français.

## Parts
Each part can be shown or hidden, put into one of five columns (1 = left edge, 3 = middle, 5 = right edge; parts in the same column line up side by side) and nudged left or right.
- **Experience**: "75% to level 33" over a thin line, the rested part shown faint. If you like, XP per hour and the time to the next level, left or right of it. While you have unspent talent points, a pulsing **Talents** appears beside it; click it to open the talent tree.
- **Gold**.
- **Durability**: your worst equipped item in %, yellow below 50, red below 25. Click to open the character window.
- **Bags**: free slots. Click to open your bags.
- **Clock**.
- **Professions**: up to four, each with its icon, name, skill and a thin line. At the skill cap the line turns gold: time for the trainer. Click to open the profession window.
- **Reputations**: up to four, each with its name, a short standing ("F" for Friendly, "H" for Honored) and a thin line. Left-click to pick the faction, right-click to open the reputation tab; a **+** adds a faction to an empty slot.

## Designs
Five looks for the lines:
- **Glass** (default): the fill glows.
- **Hairline**: one pixel; the background fades out towards the game.
- **Segments**: split like Blizzard's experience bar.
- **Icons only**: professions as icons, the values in the tooltip.
- **Alchemy phial**: tiny glass tubes with brass caps and bubbles.

## Settings
- Height, background colour and opacity.
- Accent colour, by default Forever gold (#FFCA30), and if you like a line in the accent colour along the edge towards the game.
- Professions and standings in their own colours instead of the accent.
- Hide in combat or in a group.
- A gear at the right edge of the strip opens the options.

## Icons
Its own white silhouette icons for professions, gold, durability, bags and the options gear, tinted in your accent colour.

## Commands
`/fdb` (options)
